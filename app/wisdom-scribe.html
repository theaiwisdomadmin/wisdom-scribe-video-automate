How it works

Everything on the board plays in timeline order. Each element has a camera move, a draw time, and a pause. Tick “Start new scene” on an element to wipe the board and start fresh — handy for long-form videos.

Space play/pause · Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · arrows nudge (Shift = 10×) · Esc deselect

Projects auto-save in this browser. Use Save to keep a .json file you can reopen anywhere (images, fonts and audio are bundled inside).

Voiceover: add it under Project settings (click empty board area), then press Fit timeline to voiceover to stretch or squeeze all timings to match.
